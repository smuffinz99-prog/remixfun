How it works

  1. Pick a parent. Any Solana memecoin, any remix on this site, or none if you're starting a new family.
  2. Sign one transaction. Your wallet creates the token and its metadata, sends you the full supply and permanently disables minting.
  3. Take it to market. Open a pool on Raydium or Meteora. The price shows up here automatically once it has one.
